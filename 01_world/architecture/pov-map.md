# POV Map

## Namju

- pov owner: namju
- scope: close third
- voice rules: restrained, sensory, shame-aware, slow to speak
- knows: his labor, his helplessness, what he directly observes
- must not know: sumin's hidden interior before discovery
- chapter access: allowed
- notes: strongest anchor for humiliation and authorship theft

## Sumin

- pov owner: sumin
- scope: close third
- voice rules: fragile, observant, ashamed, bodily and sensory
- knows: her guilt, exhaustion, temptation, and love
- must not know: namju's unspoken conclusions until they emerge in scene
- chapter access: allowed
- notes: strongest anchor for inward fracture and contamination

## Ultra-long-form rule

- add a new POV here before using it repeatedly in chapter planning
- define what that POV cannot know as clearly as what it can know
