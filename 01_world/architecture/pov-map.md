# POV Map

## Namju

- pov owner: namju
- scope: close third
- voice rules: restrained, sensory, shame-aware, slow to speak
- knows: his labor, his helplessness, what he directly observes
- blind spots: sumin's hidden interior, his own complicity in passivity, how broken he already is
- visible lie or self-misreading: believes endurance is still a form of agency
- must not know: sumin's exact hidden actions before discovery
- reader leverage: the reader sees the world through humiliation and delayed recognition
- chapter access: allowed
- notes: strongest anchor for authorship theft, masculine helplessness, and belated violent thought

## Sumin

- pov owner: sumin
- scope: close third
- voice rules: fragile, observant, ashamed, bodily and sensory
- knows: her guilt, exhaustion, temptation, and love
- blind spots: her self-destructive impulse, how much namju already senses, what she wants apart from escape
- visible lie or self-misreading: believes sacrifice can remain secret and contained
- must not know: namju's final conclusions until they emerge in scene
- reader leverage: the reader feels contamination and dread before they fully understand the future cost
- chapter access: allowed
- notes: strongest anchor for inward fracture, contamination, and the body's memory

## Ultra-long-form rule

- add a new POV here before using it repeatedly in chapter planning
- define what that POV cannot know as clearly as what it can know
- record the POV's blind spots and self-misreadings, not only its facts
