# Canon Laws

## Reality rules

- rule: this pilot work is realist and non-magical
- implication: no supernatural rescue, retroactive miracle, or symbolic device may break physical reality

- rule: poverty has material consequences
- implication: money, food, shelter, and treatment limits must remain narratively real

## Tone rules

- rule: tragedy must arise through accumulation, not melodramatic convenience
- implication: collapse should feel earned through pressure, guilt, and failed agency

- rule: beauty may coexist with ruin, but may not erase ruin
- implication: motifs like sunset must intensify tragedy, not sentimentalize it

## Structural non-negotiables

- rule: love between namju and sumin remains genuine until the end
- implication: the story is not "they stopped loving each other" but "love could not save them"

- rule: fake-artist is a face of structural exploitation, not just a cartoon villain
- implication: his power comes from class, status, legitimacy, and emotional coldness

- rule: failed revenge is essential to the ending
- implication: the lovers must reach the point of action and still fail to alter the world
