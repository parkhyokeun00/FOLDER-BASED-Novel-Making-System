# Structure Done Definition

This document defines when structure work is complete enough to begin writing.

## Structure is done enough when

- the story mode is chosen
- the opening promise exists
- the first volume or first shortform arc has a clear question
- the POV plan is good enough to prevent information chaos
- the first threads and first payoffs are placed
- the readiness gate passes
- the prewrite simulation passes

## Structure is not done when

- the opening still has no reader promise
- the first chapter could not be described concretely
- canon and draft claims are still mixed
- new boards are still being invented to avoid choosing a story

## Acceptable incompleteness

It is acceptable not to know:

- every later reveal
- every supporting character
- every future chapter
- every exact payoff line

It is not acceptable not to know:

- why chapter 1 exists
- what the reader should want
- what the story is about emotionally

## Warning

Overdesign is also incompleteness.

If the system keeps growing but chapter 1 is still not writable, structure work is no longer helping.
