# Pre-Chapter Sync Gate

Run this right before drafting a chapter.

Its job is to stop "draft first, sync later" drift.

## Confirm before writing

- relevant object states were reread
- active and due threads were reread
- relevant reader and traction boards were reread
- relevant parsed sources were reopened when the chapter depends on research, adaptation, or prior source filtering
- the chapter packet records the reference set that was actually used

If the chapter includes coercion, trauma, institutional failure, or contested testimony, also confirm:

- the affected character state deltas were reread
- any still-uncertain high-stakes claims are explicitly tagged
- needed legal, social, or trauma research was reopened
- glamorization or false-mutuality risk was checked before drafting

## Rule

If the chapter depends on prior facts, source material, or continuity-sensitive aftermath, do not draft from memory alone.

If this gate is skipped, note the reason explicitly.
