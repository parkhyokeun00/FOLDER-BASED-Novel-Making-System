# Chapter Acceptance Gate

Use this after a chapter is drafted and lightly revised.

Do not move on only because words now exist.

## Accept only if

- the chapter leaves real traction
- POV limits were respected
- at least one main or supporting line moved, deepened, delayed, or echoed meaningfully
- reader desire, fear, or misunderstanding changed in some way
- removing the chapter would cause a clear loss

## Reject or revise if

- the chapter closes flat
- the chapter leaks protected information
- it repeats a recent chapter's emotional function
- it exists only to carry setup without living tension
