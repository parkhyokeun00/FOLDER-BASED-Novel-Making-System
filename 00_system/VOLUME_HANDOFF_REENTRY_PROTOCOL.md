# Volume Handoff Re-entry Protocol

Use this at the end of one volume and before the opening of the next.

Its job is to protect re-entry.

## End of volume handoff

Record:

- what promise was answered
- what desire remains alive
- what fear remains active
- what the reader must remember
- what may safely fade
- what must be reintroduced naturally

## Start of next volume re-entry

Define:

- the first 30-page or first-opening-unit re-entry job
- the first reward of the new volume
- the first reminder the reader should receive
- the one thing to avoid over-explaining immediately

## Rule

Volumes are not only ended.
They are handed off.
