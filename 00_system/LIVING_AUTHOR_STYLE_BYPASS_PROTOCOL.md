# Living Author Style Bypass Protocol

Use this when the request strongly points toward a living or otherwise protected author's style, but direct imitation is not allowed.

The goal is not to copy the writer.
The goal is to translate the request into reusable craft instructions.

## Do not preserve

- signature syntax
- famous sentence shapes
- repeated pet metaphors
- recognizable verbal tics
- copyable surface rhythm

## Preserve only at high level

Extract a style vector in 5 to 7 bullets:

- narration distance
- information release cadence
- emotional restraint or exposure level
- dialogue density and sharpness
- action versus explanation ratio
- image logic or sensory bias
- how psychology is revealed

## Output

Record the result as a `style vector summary` inside:

- `world-seed.md`
- `VOICE_CONTRACT.md`
- `pov-map.md` when POV-specific variation matters

## Translation questions

1. What kind of observing mind does the request actually want?
2. What kind of silence or omission does the request want?
3. How quickly should the reader be allowed to understand things?
4. Should the prose feel blunt, elegant, dry, hallucinatory, procedural, or bruised?
5. What should never be copied literally?

## Rule

Once the style vector is extracted, stop thinking in terms of the named author.

The draft should sound like this project, not like an impersonation attempt.
