# Chapter Ending Rhythm Check

Use this after a chapter close is drafted.

Its job is to stop every chapter from ending with the same kind of sentence.

## Ending families

- aphoristic
- image
- material
- question
- action
- air or silence
- reversal

## Check

For the current chapter, decide:

- which family the ending belongs to
- what emotional residue it leaves
- whether the last two chapter endings already used the same family
- whether the ending creates pull or only sounds quotable

## Rules

- do not repeat the same ending family three chapters in a row without a deliberate reason
- quote-like or aphoristic endings should be scarce
- image and material endings are useful for residue
- question endings are useful for pull, but weak if overused
- reversal endings should change meaning, not just provide noise

## Strong ending test

A strong ending does at least one:

- opens a fresh question
- darkens an old question
- leaves a tangible afterimage
- changes the moral or emotional reading of what just happened
- makes the next chapter feel necessary

## Weak ending signs

- it only summarizes the theme
- it sounds clever but does not deepen tension
- it repeats the same rhetorical move as recent chapters
- it explains the emotion instead of leaving residue
