# Midform Case Expansion Guide

Use this when a story is too large for pure shortform compression, but still too narrow for full novel scale.

This guide is for 5 to 8 chapter works, novella-scale cases, and middle-length suspense pieces.

## Midform signs

Choose midform when two or more are true:

- the story needs discovery and verification, not only revelation
- a document, testimony, recording, or memory must first be trusted and then questioned
- the protagonist needs at least one adversarial confrontation before the main inversion
- the ending needs residue after the reveal, not just a reveal itself
- the material has one strong spine, but that spine cannot breathe in only 3 scenes

## Stay shortform when

- the story is mostly `discovery -> recognition -> strike`
- one decisive scene can carry the irreversible change
- the evidence does not need prolonged testing
- the ending does not need a long residue

## Escalate to long-form when

- there are multiple independent storylines
- more than two major POVs are required
- the social or institutional field must expand outward across many chapters
- the antagonist requires a large ecosystem rather than one concentrated pressure field

## Default 7-chapter pattern

1. shock or discovery
2. verification gap
3. first adversarial contact
4. corroboration, witness, or pattern confirmation
5. human cost or motive complication
6. inversion confrontation
7. legal, social, or emotional residue

## Midform rules

- keep one dominant spine
- keep the cast narrow
- let each chapter do a visibly different kind of work
- do not bloat into novel pacing
- do not compress so hard that the investigation becomes summary

## Practical warning

If the story naturally wants `discovery -> contaminated evidence -> confrontation -> inversion -> residue`, do not force it back into pure shortform.
