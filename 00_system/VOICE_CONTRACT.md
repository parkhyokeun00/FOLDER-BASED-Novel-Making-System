# Voice Contract

Use this document to keep prose style stable across scenes, chapters, volumes, and agents.

## Core promise

The system should not preserve canon at the cost of losing voice.
Style drift is a continuity problem.

## Default prose rules

- Prefer concrete, sensory prose over abstract explanation.
- Let gesture, image, texture, and timing carry emotion before direct labeling.
- Use sentence length deliberately. Do not let every paragraph settle into the same rhythm.
- Favor restraint over melodrama unless the volume's voice intentionally breaks.

## Sensory priority

- primary: visual light, color, shadow, spatial pressure
- secondary: touch, temperature, surface, weight
- tertiary: sound, silence, hum, interruption

## Dialogue rule

- Spoken lines should sound like the speaker, not like the system.
- Keep dialogue faithful to class, education, stress, shame, power, and concealment.
- Preserve subtext. People often say the wrong thing for the right reason.

## Reader-facing integrity rule

- Do not leave internal drafting labels in the prose.
- Avoid phrases that expose outline scaffolding such as raw chapter numbering, board references, or planning language unless the work is intentionally metafictional.

## POV variation

Each POV entry in `pov-map.md` should define:

- tonal temperature
- sensory bias
- self-knowledge level
- blindness pattern
- sentence rhythm tendency

## Volume drift rule

Voice may evolve by volume, but only intentionally.
When a volume changes the prose texture, record:

- what changed
- why it changed
- which POVs are affected

## Quick review questions

- Does this chapter sound like the same series?
- Does this POV sound like the same mind?
- Has urgency flattened the prose into generic speed?
- Has exposition erased tension or subtext?
- Has emotion become explanation instead of experience?
