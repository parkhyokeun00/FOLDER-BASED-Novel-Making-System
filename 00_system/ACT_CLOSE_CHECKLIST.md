# Act Close Checklist

Run this at the end of every major act.

## Structural closure

- Was the act's central pressure actually advanced or answered?
- Did at least one major character-state arc move?
- Did at least one relationship shift in a durable way?
- Did the act close with a changed situation, not just a stopped scene?

## Thread review

- Which threads moved from active to maturing or due?
- Which due threads must be addressed next act?
- Which new threads were opened and why?
- Is thread load still manageable?

## Reader and pacing review

- What does the reader now want most?
- What does the reader now fear most?
- What misunderstanding is currently alive?
- Does the act ending create a real reason to continue?

## Canon and planning

- Create a checkpoint.
- Record any architecture drift caused by the act.
- Update the continuity watchlist if risk appeared.
- Confirm whether the next act plan is still valid.
