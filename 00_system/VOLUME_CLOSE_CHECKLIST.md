# Volume Close Checklist

Run this at the end of every volume.

## Volume promise

- Was the volume's central question answered, even if imperfectly?
- Does this volume stand as its own emotional journey?
- Did the ending close one promise while opening a larger one?

## Arc audit

- Which character-state arcs clearly moved this volume?
- Which relationship arcs changed shape?
- Which world-conflict pressures intensified, inverted, or resolved?
- Which motifs were planted, echoed, or paid off?

## Thread audit

- How many threads were opened?
- How many were closed?
- Which due threads now roll into the next volume?
- Is the thread load heavier than the volume can justify?

## Reader audit

- What should the reader still remember at the next opening?
- What can now fade safely?
- Which controlled misunderstandings are still alive?
- What is the next volume's strongest purchase driver?

## Theme and resonance

- Did the volume express its planned theme variation?
- Did it add a new counterargument or tension to the central theme?
- Did the volume create a memorable image, line, or emotional fingerprint?

## Freeze and handoff

- Create a checkpoint.
- Update key `evolution.md` files if used.
- Sync traction, reader, and pulse boards.
- Confirm manuscript output matches the frozen state.
- Define entry conditions for the next volume.
