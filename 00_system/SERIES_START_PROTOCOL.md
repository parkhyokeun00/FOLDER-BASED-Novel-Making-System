# Series Start Protocol

Use this before launching a true multi-volume story.

## Define the series spine

- the series-wide dramatic engine
- the irreversible promise you cannot betray
- the repeatable pleasure the reader should return for
- the escalation ladder across volumes
- the terminal emotional horizon

## Define volume 001's role

- what volume 001 answers
- what volume 001 withholds
- what volume 001 makes the reader buy into emotionally
- why volume 002 must exist

## Define the carry system

- what kind of handoff each volume should leave
- what memory must survive between volumes
- what desire must sharpen between volumes
- what misreading can stay alive for more than one volume

## Rule

If this cannot be named yet, the project is not ready to behave like a series.
