# Chapter Extension Gate

Run this after a chapter draft exists but before it is accepted.

Its job is to catch chapters that stop because the packet is complete rather than because the chapter is complete.

## Ask

- does the chapter have a lived sensory center, not only a functional summary
- does the chapter earn enough dwell time for its main emotional turn
- does the handoff to the next chapter feel pulled rather than merely cut
- if this chapter handles aftermath, recovery, or human cost, does it contain at least one concrete ordinary-life anchor
- is the ending happening because the chapter is done, not because a target length was reached

## Outcomes

- `extend`: stay longer in the chapter and deepen scene presence
- `split`: the chapter is doing too much and needs a cleaner division
- `hold`: do not accept yet
- `pass`: proceed to chapter acceptance
